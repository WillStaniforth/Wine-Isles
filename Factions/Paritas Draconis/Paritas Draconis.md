Paritas Draconis is an order of metallic dragons that abide by a strict set of tenets that they believe tempers their power and stops them from abusing status as nigh-immortal beings. The members of Paritas Draconis are much more likely to live in towns or cities, intermingling with mortals than their prouder cousins.

***Iucidium Paritas.*** 
Dragons hatched within Paritas Draconis undergo an unusual rite throughout their youths. The first time that the wyrmlings take their humanoid form, usually a few days after birth, they participate in a ritual that will force them to hold this form for the first 50 years of their lives. This sealing magic cannot be unbound earlier without aid from the dragon that performed the initial ritual. 

The Paritas Draconis believes that the best way of serving mortals and remaining humble is to walk in their shoes for an extended period of time. This ritual serves as a way for young dragons to humble themselves, and to learn what life is like from their mortal perspective. 

When the first fifty years is complete, they return to the temple of their birth and make their first transformation into their true form. With their regained power of flight, they then ascend for the first time to the upper levels of the temple.

***Humanoid Forms.***
Due to spending the first 50 years of their lives in a humanoid form that will age as slowly as they will, dragons are usually born with the form of a long-lived species. Elves are the most common for these dragons to live as, although it is not uncommon for them to reside as dwarves or halflings.
