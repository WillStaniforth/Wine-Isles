Arketios (Ar-keet-ee-ohs) is one of [[Orduvanthia the Tender]]'s children, and the younger sister of [[Mylasathios]]. She is a young dragon, around 30 years old. She is a member of the [[Paritas Draconis]], unwillingly. She was born into it much like her siblings, and is still undergoing the Iudicium Paritas. As such, she is permanently in her humanoid form; that of a young sea elven woman.

She is highly resentful of being trapped in her elven form, and seeks a way out early. She would be a great boon to any that could help free her.
