
The Southern Outpost of Isla Cereme is a large compound that handles the management of the island. It is at all times staffed by dozens of workers and rangers. 

This site is overseen by Labriss, a Mind Flayer Arcanist that is in a perpetual disguise self, appearing as a heavily bearded human. Tressk wears an amulet that grants access to the wall of force surrounding Lord Vossk's manor.
