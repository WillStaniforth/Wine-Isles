
The Northern Outpost of Isla Cereme is a large compound where various creatures are brought before they are released into the island. Whenever a PC uses a telespork to teleport something, it arrives here.

This site is overseen by Tressk, a Mind Flayer Arcanist that is in a perpetual disguise self, appearing as a heavily bearded human. Tressk wears an amulet that grants access to the wall of force surrounding Lord Vossk's manor.
