

# The Scrimshaw Shack
*Music - 19 Gambling Glottis*


"Tomorrow night, there's a ship coming into port. 'The High Roller', luxury casino-ship, all sort of prim-an-proper-types n' shit."

Your colleague, *Membrillo*, stands at the table tankard in his calloused dwarven hand.

"It'll only be 'ere a few hours, restock on booze n' bar snacks n' stuff"

The sound of rain pelting the roof is almost drowned out by the revelry of the sailors merry-making in the small harbourside tavern.

"This is an opportunity for us, see. There's a vault on that ship that's gonna be *full to bursting*. If we can sneak you in there, we'll be as rich as Max himself!"

The air is thick with the smell of ale, sea salt, and the stale sweat of sailors. You're still not quite sure how you can tell.

"Once you've got the gold, you just need to go all 'man overboard', and walk it back!"



# Leaving the Shack
*Music - 16 By the Lighthouse*

Leaving the *Scrimshaw Shack*, you begin to make your way through the dark streets of Maximino harbour. Night fell hours ago, but the lights of the casinos still shines in the distance. The gloom of the harbour is only made worse by the sheets of rain. As you make your way closer to the port of the High Roller, something shining catches your eye from an alley. Before you know it, a group of ratfolk brandishing daggers have you surrounded.

"'Ere now lads, look who we've found 'ere! Looks like Spitmeet's gonna get 'is pay after all....you couldn't hide from Spitmeet forever, shitflek!" one of the ratfolk spits towards Membrillo.

# Dock 3
*Music - 23 Rubacava*

Overlooked by the grand Casino Maximino rising high to the west, this marble harbour has seen it's better years. While maintaining an air of elegance, black stains and dirtying have left it a shadow of what it once was.

Docked in this harbour is a grand boat, decorative fin-like sails jutting from its side evoking the designs of the airships of the north. Behind the fins, each side carries a huge wooden wheel to propel the ship at great speed without being reliant on the wind. The vessel is painted red, with beautiful red-tile canopied buildings atop it's deck. You can even see the top of a palm tree on the deck.
