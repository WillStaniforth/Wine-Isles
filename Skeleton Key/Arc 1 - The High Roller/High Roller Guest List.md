
# **Lord Fystook Wyrnralei**
A young bronze dragonborn bachelor, 2nd son of Lord Lilkyr Wyrnralei of House Wyrnralei. 
He has a short snout and a horned a horned crest, with piercing blue eyes. His scales are a shiny bronze with no burnishing to him indicating he is rather young.

Fystook is aboard to make as much money as quickly as possible, and make potential new trade allies.

**Personality Trait**s:
I take great paints to always look my best and follow the latest fashions. I always wear a fancy hat. I am secretly a coward.

**Ideal:** Independence
I must prove that I can handle myself without coddling from my family. (Chaotic)

**Bond:** 
I must preserve my wealth at all costs. With [[Orduvanthia the Tender]] missing and my family's seat of power at risk, I must take drastic action.

**Flaw:** 
I secretly believe everyone is beneath me. In fact, the world does revolve around me.
